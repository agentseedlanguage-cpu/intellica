# Software Architecture Academy — research and competitive review

**Review date:** 25 November 2026  
**Baseline:** Architecture Academy checkpoint 36. The existing six-level pathway, course content, mock-exam bank and architecture-specific pages are preserved; this review updates public positioning and records the most relevant external benchmarks.

## Executive position

Intellica's Software Architecture Academy is an independent professional learning pathway. Its current courses provide a strong practical sequence:

- Level 1: Software Architecture Foundations — 14 lessons, six modules, 112 slides, 70 recall questions, one 14-section architecture artefact lab and three original timed mock exams.
- Level 2: Mastering arc42 — practical architecture documentation using an open template.
- Level 3: Quality, Decisions and Evaluation — 20 lessons and a cumulative quality/decision record.

Levels 4–6 remain planned. Their public pages must show intended outcomes without implying that their courses or credentials are available. The current Level 4 scope should explicitly acknowledge the new iSAQB CPSA-A AGENTA module as an external benchmark, while keeping Intellica's curriculum and credential independent.

## Relevant standards and academic foundations

### ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022

The current edition specifies requirements for architecture descriptions and the supporting architecture-description framework, architecture-description language, viewpoints and model kinds. It distinguishes an architecture from the description of that architecture. It does not prescribe the process, method, notation or tool by which an architecture description is created.

**Curriculum implication:** Level 2 should teach arc42 as a practical open template for organising architecture information, while distinguishing template use from formal conformance to ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010. The learner should be able to identify stakeholders, concerns, viewpoints, model kinds and correspondence between views, not merely fill in headings.

### arc42

arc42 is an open, practical template for documenting software and system architecture. It is a documentation aid, not a professional certification or an accreditation scheme. It can support an architecture description, but using it does not automatically establish ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 conformance.

**Curriculum implication:** preserve the cumulative artefact approach: each lesson adds a real section to one architecture description, and the learner can export and defend it.

### iSAQB Certified Professional for Software Architecture (CPSA)

- The current official CPSA-F curriculum is version 2025.1 (valid from 1 April 2025). The public iSAQB portal now also lists a 2027.1 release candidate, scheduled to become valid on 1 April 2027; Intellica should version its mapping before that date.
- The CPSA-A programme is modular: the current public catalogue lists 20 modules organised around technological, methodical and communicative competence.
- The CPSA-A exam requires at least 70 credit points across the three areas, with at least 10 points in each. The AGENTA module awards 10 technological and 20 methodical credit points. Candidates submit a term paper reviewed by two recognised examiners and defend it orally.
- The CPSA-A module catalogue now includes **AGENTA — Architecture for Agentic Software Engineering Contexts**, a direct external benchmark for Intellica Level 4.

**Curriculum implication:** Level 1 should maintain its versioned CPSA-F topic map and original mock exams, then refresh against the 2027.1 curriculum before 1 April 2027. Level 4 should be mapped against AGENTA's public learning outcomes and credit allocation; the current page now identifies AGENTA as an external reference without claiming iSAQB credit or accreditation. Do not claim iSAQB equivalence, accreditation or provider status.

### The Open Group TOGAF Standard, 10th Edition

The Open Group offers TOGAF Enterprise Architecture Foundation and Practitioner credentials. Foundation focuses on knowledge and comprehension; Practitioner assesses application and analysis. The Open Group maintains a register of accredited training courses/providers.

**Curriculum implication:** Level 5–6 may use TOGAF as an adjacent enterprise-architecture benchmark for governance, operating models, transformation and stakeholder alignment. It is not a substitute for software architecture design, quality analysis or engineering evidence.

### Carnegie Mellon Software Engineering Institute (SEI)

The SEI Software Architecture Professional Certificate is earned through a sequence of architecture courses and an examination. The SEI explicitly states its training courses are not academic courses for degree credit and that its certificates are not official academic credentials.

**Curriculum implication:** benchmark the breadth of principles, documentation, design and analysis, and the use of an examination. Intellica should differentiate through cumulative learner artefacts, guided labs, versioned source traceability and explicit assessment evidence rather than claiming superiority by assertion.

## Competitive and accreditation landscape

| Benchmark | What it validates | How Intellica should use it |
|---|---|---|
| iSAQB CPSA-F 2025.1 | Foundation-level software architecture knowledge and terminology | Maintain the Level 1 topic map and original practice exams; disclose that Intellica is not an accredited provider. |
| iSAQB CPSA-A | Advanced methodical, technological and communicative competence; term paper and oral defence | Benchmark Level 3–6 assessment depth, portfolio evidence and oral defence. |
| iSAQB AGENTA | Architecture for agentic software engineering contexts | Direct benchmark for Level 4 Agentic AI Architecture; update the lesson map after reviewing the official module objectives. |
| TOGAF 10th Edition Foundation / Practitioner | Enterprise architecture concepts and applied method | Adjacent benchmark for Levels 5–6 governance, transformation and enterprise leadership. |
| SEI Software Architecture Professional Certificate | Principles, documentation, design/analysis and examination sequence | Benchmark for breadth and the relationship between instruction and assessment. |
| ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022 | Architecture-description requirements, viewpoints and model kinds | Normative reference for documentation quality and viewpoint completeness; not a design method. |
| arc42 | Open template for practical architecture documentation | Core documentation tool for Level 2; not a credential or proof of standards conformity. |
| ISO/IEC 17024:2026 | Requirements for bodies certifying persons | Future scheme-design reference only. Intellica is not accredited and must not imply conformity. |

## Improvements recorded in the current repository

1. The main Training page now presents both professional academies and makes the distinction between available courses and planned levels explicit.
2. The Architecture Academy page now uses professional programme language, publishes the status of each level and explains the limits of its external alignment.
3. Level 4 Agentic AI Architecture now identifies iSAQB CPSA-A AGENTA as a relevant benchmark without claiming equivalence or accreditation.
4. The Mastering arc42 course description now distinguishes the open template from the requirements in ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010:2022.
5. The training-wide learning method now cites research on retrieval, spacing, active learning, worked examples and feedback.

## Recommended next architecture curriculum work

- Obtain and review the current AGENTA module curriculum and map each learning outcome to a lesson, lab and assessment evidence item.
- Build Level 4 around agent/workflow selection, tool contracts, authority boundaries, memory/context architecture, evaluation, observability, safety and secure architecture documentation.
- Extend Level 5 with architecture governance, fitness functions, architecture decision records, portfolio-level risk and evolutionary change.
- Extend Level 6 with enterprise strategy, operating models, organisational change, stakeholder communication, investment prioritisation and responsible AI governance.
- Maintain the architecture artefact as a cumulative learner portfolio and add cross-level review gates.
- Use ISO/IEC/IEEE 42010 as a documentation-quality reference, not as a substitute for architectural reasoning.
- Keep all external exam preparation based on public objectives and original Intellica questions.
